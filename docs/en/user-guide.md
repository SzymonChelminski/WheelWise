# WheelWise user guide

This guide shows how to use WheelWise. Names in quotation marks are the labels that you see on the site in English.

## 1. What WheelWise is for

WheelWise shows what a new car costs after it leaves the showroom. For each car it adds up depreciation, fuel or electricity, insurance, servicing, parts and wear, and fees. You can rank cars, compare two or three of them, and see the net income at which a car fits your budget.

The site is for drivers in Poland. All amounts are in zloty (zł). The site covers new cars only.

The cars and their prices come from French market data (an open dataset of a French government agency). WheelWise converts the French price to zloty with the current exchange rate and the Polish VAT. This is why every price on the site is marked "indicative price". Cars are compared on the same basis, so the order in the rankings is still useful. The price at a Polish dealer can differ.

There are no accounts. You do not log in, and the site does not save cars or comparisons on a server.

## 2. Find the cheapest cars: rankings

Open "Rankings" in the top menu. The page shows a list of cars, from the lowest value to the highest.

**Choose what to rank by.** Use "Rank by". There are nine rankings:

| Ranking | What it shows |
|---|---|
| "Purchase price" | The cheapest new cars by indicative price. |
| "Total cost" | Every cost added up, depreciation included. This is the ranking that opens first. |
| "Depreciation" | Which cars lose value most slowly. |
| "Maintenance" | Servicing, parts and fees together. |
| "Servicing" | Inspections and scheduled service. |
| "Parts and wear" | Tyres, brakes and other wear parts. |
| "Fuel consumption" | Liters per 100 km, WLTP figures. |
| "Fuel cost" | Fuel or electricity at your mileage. |
| "Insurance" | Third-party or comprehensive, for your age. |

In the "Fuel consumption" ranking, plug-in hybrids are left out, because their consumption depends on how often you charge them. Electric cars appear in kWh per 100 km when "electric" is the only powertrain that you selected in the filters.

**Filter the list.** On a wide screen the filters are on the left. On a phone, tap "Filters" to open them, and tap the button at the bottom ("Show 25 cars", with the real number) to close them. You can filter by:

- "Make or model": a free text. Case and accents do not matter. Every word must match.
- "Indicative price": "From" and "To".
- "Body type", "Powertrain", "Segment" and "Make": select one or more.
- "Gearbox": "Any", "Manual" or "Automatic".

The list "Active filters" shows what is on. Use "Remove filter" to switch one off, or "Clear filters" to switch all off. If no car matches, you see "No car matches these filters". Widen the price range or switch some filters off.

**Search for one car.** On the home page, type in "Search for a car" (for example "Clio"). Pick a suggestion to open the page of the car. If there is no match, you see "We don’t have that car". Check the spelling or type the make only.

**Show more.** The list shows 10 cars. Select "Show 10 more" to add the next ten. A line such as "Showing 10 of 489" tells you how many cars the list holds.

The home page shows two short lists: "Cheapest to buy" and "Lowest total cost". Use "All rankings" to go to the other rankings.

## 3. Set your own assumptions

The costs depend on how you use the car. The block "Assumptions" shows your current values, for example "15,000 km a year, 5 years, OC + AC, driver aged 35–44". Select "Change" to edit them, and "Done" to close the panel. On the car page on a wide screen, the panel is always visible on the right.

| Setting | What to enter | Default |
|---|---|---|
| "Yearly mileage" | From 1,000 to 100,000 km, digits only. | 15,000 km |
| "How long you will keep the car" | From 1 to 10 years. | 5 years |
| "Insurance" | "Estimate it for me" or "I know my premium". | "Estimate it for me" |
| "Driver’s age" | An age band from "18–24 years" to "75 years and over". Only with "Estimate it for me". | "35–44 years" |
| "Cover" | "OC only" (third-party) or "OC + AC" (third-party and comprehensive). Only with "Estimate it for me". | "OC + AC" |
| "Yearly premium" | The amount from your policy or quote, for a full year, from 0 to 100,000 zł. Only with "I know my premium". | none |

The new values apply at once. If you type a value that is not allowed, you see a message such as "Enter a mileage from 1,000 to 100,000 km, digits only.", and the old value stays in use.

**Where the site remembers your choices.** The values are kept in the address of the page and in your browser. When you open the site again in the same browser, it takes the last values. You can send a link to another person, and that person sees the same values. The site has no account, and it does not keep your choices in its database. If you use another browser or clear the data of the site, the choices are gone.

## 4. Monthly or yearly view

Use the switch "Cost period" to choose "Monthly" or "Yearly". All costs and the income amounts change together. A yearly amount is 12 times the monthly amount. The default is "Monthly".

The switch is on the rankings, the car page and the comparison. It does not appear for the rankings "Purchase price" and "Fuel consumption", because those values do not depend on the period.

## 5. Read the car page

Select a car in a list to open its page. The page shows:

- The name of the car and its price with the tag "indicative price". Below it you see the date of the price list ("Price list of ...") and a link "source: ADEME Car Labelling". The link opens the open dataset where the price comes from.
- The details of the car: "Body type", "Segment", "Powertrain", "Gearbox", power, "Engine size" and "Consumption (WLTP)". For hybrid cars the power is the power of the combustion engine only. For electric cars it is the power in the source data, usually the rated power, which is lower than the peak power that the manufacturer quotes.
- "Cost of ownership": the total cost for the period that you chose, and a table "Costs by category" with the rows "Depreciation", "Fuel and energy", "Insurance", "Servicing", "Parts and wear", "Fees" and "Total". The column "Share" shows the part of each category in the total.
- Two lines under the table: what the car will be worth at the end of your period ("After 5 years the car will be worth ... (... of the price)") and what every kilometer costs.
- "The income this car calls for": the earnings scale (section 6).
- A button "Compare" that adds the car to a comparison (section 7).

The sign ≈ before an amount means an estimate. The price is not an offer. The link "How we calculate" opens the methodology page, which explains each number.

The purchase price is not added to the total cost. Depreciation takes its place: it is the part of the price that you do not get back when you sell the car. The site assumes that you pay in cash and it does not count loans, leasing, parking, tolls, fines, washing or tyre storage.

The name and the price belong to the cheapest version of the car. Versions that differ only in drive, battery size, number of seats or body length count as one car. They cost more.

## 6. Earnings scale

The section "The income this car calls for" shows how much net income you need so that the car does not take too much of your budget. Net income is the money that you get after taxes, "take-home pay". The amounts are monthly in the "Monthly" view and yearly in the "Yearly" view.

The scale has four levels. The share is the part of your net income that the total cost of the car takes:

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| "comfortable" | the car takes up to 10% of income |
| "reasonable" | the car takes 10–15% of income |
| "stretching it" | the car takes 15–20% of income |
| "not recommended" | the car takes over 20% of income |

Each level has a range of income:

- "from X" means an income of X or more.
- "from X to Y" means an income between X and Y.
- "up to Y" means an income of Y or less. This is the last level, "not recommended". If your net income is in this range, the total cost of the car is above 20% of it, and the site advises against the car.

Example for a total cost of 2,000 zł per month: "comfortable" starts at 20,000 zł, "reasonable" is 13,300 to 20,000 zł, "stretching it" is 10,000 to 13,300 zł, and "not recommended" is up to 10,000 zł. The amounts are rounded to 100 zł.

The scale follows the 20/4/10 rule and common advice of financial advisers. It uses the total monthly cost with depreciation. It is a guide, not a financial decision.

## 7. Compare two or three cars

1. Select "Compare" on a car in a ranking or on the car page. The button changes to "In comparison".
2. A bar at the bottom of the page shows "Selected: 2 of 3". Select the button "Compare 2 cars" in the bar, or select "Compare" in the top menu.
3. The page "Car comparison" shows a table with the cars side by side: price, value after your period, consumption, power and each cost category. For each cost, the lowest value is marked "lowest" and the highest is marked "highest".

Details:

- A comparison holds at most 3 cars. When you try to add a fourth, you see "A comparison holds 3 cars. Remove one to add another."
- You need at least two cars for a table. With fewer, the page shows "Add at least 2 cars" or "Add one more car".
- On the comparison page, use the search field to add a car ("Add a third car"), and "Remove" to take a car out. In the bar, "Clear" removes all cars.
- The comparison uses your assumptions and the period switch. Change them and the table changes.
- Your selection stays in your browser. The address of the comparison page holds the cars, so you can send the link.

## 8. Change the language

Select "PL" or "EN" in the top bar to switch between Polish and English. You stay on the same page, and your assumptions and selected cars stay as they are. When you open the site for the first time, it uses the language of your browser: English if your browser is set to English, otherwise Polish. The site remembers your last choice.

## 9. Limits of the data

- **The numbers are estimates.** Depreciation, insurance, servicing, parts and fees come from a model with parameters based on published market averages. No open data exists for these values per car model. The sign ≈ marks an estimate. For insurance you can enter your own premium.
- **Prices are indicative.** They are French list prices converted to zloty, with French VAT (20%) replaced by Polish VAT (23%), rounded to 100 zł. Manufacturers set prices country by country. The French ecological bonus and malus are not included. Check the price with a dealer.
- **The range of cars is the French one.** The catalogue has 37 makes. Some cars sold in Poland are missing. Some versions sold only in France are there.
- **Consumption is the WLTP value.** It is usually lower than in everyday driving, plug-in hybrids especially. The real fuel cost is likely to be higher than shown.
- **How often the data changes.** The site checks all sources once a day. Fuel prices come from a weekly source, the car price list from a source that updates about every quarter, the electricity price every half-year, and the exchange rate on working days. The footer shows when the data was checked and the date of each price list. The date next to a car price is the day that the source updated its data, not the day of the check.
- **Names.** Car names come from the source. They can be in capital letters or contain French words. Some names are shared by two cars, mostly the manual and the automatic version. The lists always show the body type, powertrain, gearbox and power next to the name.
- **Browser.** The site needs JavaScript. The Back button of the browser goes back to the previous page, not to the previous filter.
- **Not advice.** WheelWise is neither a sales offer nor financial or insurance advice. Before you buy, check the price with a dealer and ask for an insurance quote.
